# Welcome to Stacks Wars

**Stacks Wars** is a decentralized competitive gaming platform built on the Stacks blockchain. Compete against players worldwide, deposit STX securely in our vault, and claim your winnings through cryptographic signatures.

## How It Works

The Stacks Wars experience follows a simple but powerful flow:

1. **Authenticate** - Connect your Leather or Xverse wallet to get started
2. **Join or Create** - Find a lobby or create your own game session
3. **Deposit STX** - Transfer your stake to our secure Vault contract
4. **Play** - Compete in Lexi Wars or Ludo with other players
5. **Claim** - The server signs the game result and you claim your winnings
