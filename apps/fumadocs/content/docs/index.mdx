# Welcome to Stacks Wars

**Stacks Wars** is a decentralized competitive gaming platform built on the Stacks blockchain. Compete against players worldwide, wager STX or fungible tokens securely through smart contract vaults, and claim your winnings with cryptographic signature verification.

## How It Works

1. **Authenticate** — Connect your Leather or Xverse wallet
2. **Join or Create** — Find a lobby or create your own game session
3. **Deposit** — Your stake is held in a smart contract vault (escrow)
4. **Play** — Compete in Lexi Wars, Ludo, or community-built games
5. **Claim** — The server signs the result and you claim your winnings on-chain

## Platform Features

- **On-Chain Wagering** — STX and fungible token support via audited vault contracts
- **Cryptographic Claim System** — Server-signed results verified on-chain
- **Leaderboards & Seasons** — Earn Wars Points and climb the seasonal rankings
- **Trust Rating** — Build your reputation through consistent fair play
- **Live Chat & Reactions** — Communicate with opponents during games
- **Sponsored Lobbies** — Risk-free play funded by sponsors
- **Plugin System** — Extensible game engine for community-built games
