## Wars Points System

**Wars Points** measure your competitive standing across all games and lobbies.

### Earning Points

You earn points based on:

| Action | Points | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Win a game | 10-50 | More points for harder opponents |
| Play a game | 2 | Participation points |
| Wager match | +10% bonus | Win with higher wager |
| Win streak | 2x multiplier | 3+ consecutive wins |
| Fair play | +5 | Trust Rating bonus |

**Example:**
```
You beat 3 opponents in a 10 STX Lexi Wars lobby:
- Base win: 25 points
- Wager bonus: +2.5 points  
- Trust bonus: +5 points
- Total: 32.5 points per round
```

### Losing Points

You don't lose points for losses, but inactivity resets streaks:

- **Losing a game:** 0 points (no penalty)
- **Not playing for 7 days:** Streak resets
- **Forfeiting:** -10 points (discouraged but allowed)

## Trust Rating

Every player has a **Trust Rating** (0-100) that prevents cheating and rewards honest play.

### What Trust Rating Measures

**Fair Play:**
- Completing games (not rage quitting)
- Following game rules
- No exploit attempts
- No wallet manipulation

**Reliability:**
- Claiming winnings on time
- Quick transaction confirmations
- No dispute escalations
- Consistent participation

**Community Standing:**
- Reports from other players (verified)
- Mod reviews of flagged games
- Dispute resolution outcomes

### Your Trust Score

- **90-100:** Legendary (VIP tournament access)
- **75-89:** Trusted (priority matchmaking)
- **50-74:** Neutral (standard play)
- **25-49:** Caution (limited to lower stakes)
- **0-24:** Restricted (limited to Sponsored lobbies)

### How to Improve Trust Rating

1. **Complete every game** (even if you lose)
2. **Claim winnings quickly** (within 24 hours)
3. **Play fair** (no exploits, no cheating)
4. **Help the community** (report actual issues)
5. **Maintain consistency** (play regularly)

### Trust Rating Penalties

- **Rage quitting:** -5 points (once per season)
- **Unresolved disputes:** -10 points
- **Reported cheating (confirmed):** -25 points
- **Account compromise:** Auto-reset + recovery protocol

## Seasonal Leaderboards

Stacks Wars runs **seasonal competitions** with reset points and special rewards.

### Season Structure

| Timeframe | Duration | Typical Dates |
|-----------|----------|----------------|
| Spring | 3 months | March - May |
| Summer | 3 months | June - August |
| Fall | 3 months | September - November |
| Winter | 3 months | December - February |

### Seasonal Rewards

**Top 100 Players:**
- Prize pool: 500-1000 STX per season
- Distributed by rank (1st gets more)
- Paid out within 7 days of season end

**Top 10 Players:**
- Exclusive badge: "Legend of [Season]"
- VIP tournament invites
- Bonus 50 STX prize
- Featured on hall of fame

**Everyone Who Plays:**
- Seasonal participation badge
- Snapshot of your best rank
- Stats carry to next season (optional)

### Leaderboard Rankings

The global leaderboard shows:

1. **Your rank** (position among all players)
2. **Your Wars Points** (seasonal total)
3. **Win rate** (% of games won)
4. **Trust Rating** (0-100 score)
5. **Games played** (seasonal count)
6. **Favorite game** (Lexi Wars or Ludo)

## Leaderboard Tiers

Players are grouped into **tier brackets** for fairer competition.

### How Tiers Work

| Tier | Wars Points | Typical Opponents |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Rookie | 0-100 | New players |
| Amateur | 101-500 | Regulars |
| Competitor | 501-1000 | Dedicated players |
| Elite | 1001-2000 | Expert players |
| Legendary | 2000+ | Top 1% |

**Tier Matching:**
- Lobbies preferentially match similar tier players
- Prevents new players facing veterans
- Bonus points for "tier up" wins
- Balanced competitive experience

## Preventing Cheating

The Trust Rating system includes built-in anti-cheat:

**Anomaly Detection:**
- Unusual win patterns flagged
- Statistical analysis of outcomes
- Automated alerts for suspicious activity

**Human Review:**
- Mods review flagged accounts
- Game replay analysis available
- Fair judgment process

**Blockchain Verification:**
- All game outcomes recorded on-chain
- Smart contracts validate winners
- Immutable proof of fair play

**Community Reports:**
- Other players can report suspected cheating
- Mods investigate reports
- Confirmed cheaters lose Trust Rating instantly

## Season Reset

At the end of each season:

1. **Final payouts** are distributed to top players
2. **Your Wars Points reset to 0** (new season)
3. **Your Trust Rating persists** (doesn't reset)
4. **All-time stats** are archived and visible
5. **New season challenges** begin

### Carrying Forward

- Trust Rating is permanent (only resets on major violations)
- All-time stats are always visible on your profile
- Previous season badges remain in your collection
- Points are seasonal (fresh start each time)

## FAQ

**Q: Do I lose points for losses?**  
A: No, you earn 0 points for losses but don't lose existing points. Only forfeits cost points.

**Q: How is rank calculated?**  
A: Rank = Your position when sorted by Wars Points (highest first). Tiebreaker is join date (earlier is better).

**Q: Can I improve my Trust Rating if it's low?**  
A: Yes! Play fair, complete games, claim winnings promptly, and it will gradually improve over 4-8 weeks.

**Q: What happens if my Trust Rating hits 0?**  
A: You're restricted to Sponsored lobbies (no wagering) until your rating improves.

**Q: Do seasonal points carry over?**  
A: No, Wars Points reset each season, but all-time stats are preserved.

**Q: Are leaderboards per-game or global?**  
A: Global leaderboards mix Lexi Wars and Ludo players. Game-specific leaderboards coming soon!
