# Getting Started with Stacks Wars

Follow these steps to set up your account and start playing.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- A **Leather** or **Xverse** wallet installed on your browser
- STX tokens to deposit (test STX on testnet is fine for trying it out)
- A supported browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)

### Install a Wallet

#### Leather Wallet

1. Visit [leather.io](https://leather.io)
2. Click "Install Leather" and follow the browser extension setup
3. Create a new wallet or import an existing one
4. Save your recovery phrase securely

#### Xverse Wallet

1. Visit [xverse.app](https://xverse.app)
2. Download the extension for your browser
3. Complete the wallet setup process
4. Store your seed phrase safely

## Creating Your Account

### Step 1: Connect Your Wallet

1. Visit the Stacks Wars platform at [stacks-wars.com](https://stacks-wars.com)
2. Click the **"Connect Wallet"** button
3. Choose either **Leather** or **Xverse**
4. Your wallet extension will pop up - review the connection request
5. Click **"Connect"** to authorize the connection

### Step 2: Complete Your Profile

After wallet connection, you'll see the profile creation form:

- **Username** - Choose a unique username (3-20 characters, alphanumeric)
- **Avatar** - Upload a profile picture or select a default avatar
- **Bio** (optional) - Tell other players about yourself

Click **"Create Profile"** to finish setup.

### Step 3: Explore the Lobby

You're now ready to play!

- View the **Leaderboards** to see top players and their Wars Points
- Check **Active Games** to find lobbies you can join
- Click **"Create Lobby"** to start your own game session

## Depositing STX

When you join a game, you'll need to deposit your stake:

1. Click **"Join Game"** on a lobby
2. Enter your **bet amount** in STX
3. Click **"Approve Deposit"** - your wallet will ask for transaction confirmation
4. Your STX is transferred to the Vault contract and held securely
5. Once confirmed, the game begins!

## Claiming Your Winnings

After you win a game:

1. The server generates a **signed result** proving you won
2. Click **"Claim Prize"** on the game results screen
3. Review the amount and confirm in your wallet
4. Your winnings are transferred to your wallet instantly

> **Note:** With Stacks Nakamoto fast blocks, transactions typically confirm in ~5 seconds.

## Troubleshooting

**Q: My wallet won't connect**

- Ensure your wallet extension is installed and unlocked
- Try refreshing the page
- Check that you're using a supported browser

**Q: I don't see my deposited STX**

- STX deposits confirm in ~5 seconds with Nakamoto fast blocks
- Check the transaction status on [Explorer.Stacks.co](https://explorer.stacks.co)
- Contact support if it takes longer than 2 minutes

**Q: Can I withdraw my funds?**

- You can only withdraw unclaimed deposits by canceling a pending game
- Winnings claimed through the vault are final
